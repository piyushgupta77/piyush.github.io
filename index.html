Family Travel Tips

🍼

For the little ones

  • Pack formula, diapers, and baby food — available in Big C supermarket Da Nang
  • Most hotels provide cribs & baby bathtubs free
  • Infants fly free on domestic legs
🛡️

Practical stuff

  • Apply for e-visa online (25 USD each, 3–5 days processing)
  • Download Grab app for taxis (cheaper than airport taxis)
  • Bring mosquito patches (not spray for baby)
